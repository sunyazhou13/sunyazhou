## About Me

> Our lives are like processes in an operating system: daily activities are the threads, running on round-robin scheduling in the queue `com.sunyazhou.self.queue.serial`. I sum up my learning journey as **a lonely thread** — always executing serially: `English`, `Math`, `all sorts of things...`. To record what is easily forgotten, I created **The Mr. Sun's Work Notes** — an open-source toolbox of sorts, to revisit often and to share with those who need it.

I'm **Sun Yazhou**, from [Harbin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbin), the Ice City of northern China. I've long been an iOS application developer with a stint in macOS development — the field I'm best at among all my skills.

## 📝 Why I Blog

Blogging grew out of the evolution of how I store knowledge:

**Stack → Heap → References → Keyword Index → Blog**

At first my brain was an empty stack, pushing knowledge in until stack overflow; switching to heap storage, I ran out of [memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_of_memory); then I tried reference-tag storage, until my browser tabs multiplied like a virus; finally I kept only keywords to search with, only to find many articles had been deleted by their authors. Meanwhile I had my own technical experience worth sharing, so I decided to start this blog.

> Even if my views are full of narrow theories and naive arguments, I'll keep doing it, knowing today is right where yesterday was wrong. Otherwise I'd regret the wasted years and be ashamed of accomplishing nothing.

Blogging keeps me calm and focused on my own field, away from restlessness. I share technology and life occasionally, without obsessing over quality — after all, I also need to spend time working and living well.
