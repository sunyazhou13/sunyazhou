Manage SQLite databases right in your browser: create a new database, open `.sqlite` / `.db` files, run SQL, browse and edit table data, import and export CSV and SQL. Powered by **sql.js** (SQLite compiled to WebAssembly). **Everything runs locally in your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server**.
